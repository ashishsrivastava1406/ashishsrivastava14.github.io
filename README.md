# Product case studies

**[Read them here →](https://ashishsrivastava1406.github.io/ashishsrivastava14.github.io/)**

I'm a senior product manager at Flutter Entertainment, working on consumer
growth, monetisation and platform across India, the UK and Ireland. These are
write-ups of the product decisions I've taken, the alternatives I considered,
and what the measurement showed afterwards.

## How each one is structured

Every case study follows the same shape, so they can be compared:

1. **The calls** — the decisions the project turned on, one line each
2. **Scope** — what I owned, and what partner teams owned
3. **The constraint** — the structural problem, not the schedule
4. **What I turned down** — the alternatives, and why each lost
5. **What the measurement showed** — including the results that went against
   the decision
6. **What I would do differently**

The order is deliberate. The decisions come first and the data is the check on
them, rather than a narrative worked backwards from a dashboard.

## Notes on figures

Figures are normalised. Relative movement only: multiples and percentage point
changes, never absolute revenue, customer counts or per-brand retention rates
from a current or former employer. Screenshots have player handles, balances and
promotional codes redacted.

Where a conclusion is my reading of the data rather than something the analysis
stated outright, the write-up says so.

## Scope of my current role

- A consumer category grown roughly 20x in monthly gross profit over three
  years, on repeat purchase rather than acquisition spend
- The multitenant platform running four consumer brands across three markets off
  one codebase, and the admin console the brand teams operate it through
- The four monetisation models that replaced the revenue the ban removed
- Four product managers and two product analysts, plus the quarterly roadmap,
  prioritisation and experiment design
