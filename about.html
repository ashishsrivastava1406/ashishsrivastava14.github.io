About

Risk, then platforms, then product. Each move brought the decisions closer.

The arc

I started in risk at Amazon, investigating how people abuse a marketplace. That taught me to read behaviour from data before believing a story about it.

Platforms came next, at Affle and ABP News, where the product was infrastructure and the users were internal. Then Cars24, where the decision was a credit policy rather than a screen. Since 2022 I have been at Flutter, running a consumer category and the platform four brands sit on, and managing a team.

The through line is that each move brought the decisions closer to me: from detecting what happened, to building what others operated, to choosing what gets built and being answerable for whether it worked.
