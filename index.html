Case studies from seven years of consumer product work.

I'm a senior product manager at Flutter Entertainment, working on consumer growth, monetisation and platform products across India, the UK and Ireland. Each case study focuses on the constraint, the decision I took, the alternatives I considered, and what the measurement showed — including the times it went against the decision.

Scope

Category

I own a consumer category that has grown roughly 20x in monthly gross profit over three years, driven by repeat purchase rather than acquisition spend.

Platform

I set the vision for the multitenant platform that runs four consumer brands across India, the UK and Ireland from one codebase, and own the Junglee Rummy admin panel end to end, including its access management.

Monetisation

After India's real money gaming ban removed the existing revenue model, I chose and sequenced the four models that replaced it: virtual currency, rewarded advertising, in-app purchases and the platform's first subscription.

Leadership

I manage four product managers and two product analysts, and own the quarterly roadmap, prioritisation and experiment design across the portfolio.
