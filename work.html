Work

Five case studies, each covering the constraint, the decision, the alternatives considered, and what the measurement showed.

Discovery2024 to 2025

Tournament Lobby Revamp

Rebuilding the tournament lobby on segment-level sorting and configurable merchandising, tested three ways so the personalisation layer could be measured apart from the card redesign.

ARPU rose about seven percent — from the redesign, not the personalisation.

Read case study

Ranking2024

Tournament Recommendations

A recommendation engine built on three separate persona logics rather than one model, including a route that inferred tournament affinity from a player's ring-game spend.

Tournament revenue per user up 7.3 percent, participation flat.

Read case study

Acquisition2024

Tournament Ingress

Entry points into tournaments from the cash and practice surfaces players already used, including mid-game, and the restraints that kept an intrusive surface from damaging the game it sat on.

Players doing both rose from about 35 to about 40 percent.

Read case study

Gameplay2024 to 2025

Bot Perception

Players believed they were facing bots. They were facing registered players who never took their seat. Rebuilding tournament matchmaking rather than addressing the belief.

Away instances in round one fell from 21.4 to 6.1 percent.

Read case study

Lifecycle2024

Reactivation on Live Signals

Replacing scheduled ticket giveaways with incentives triggered by observed drop-off, deliberately delayed so the business does not pay for conversions that were already coming.

Join success up 1.33 points; ARPU up 0.22 percent once outliers are treated.
